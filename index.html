About Me

I was born in Baghdad, Iraq. I received a B.Sc. degree in Information Technology from Middle Technical University, Baghdad, Iraq, an M.Sc. degree in Computer Science from Sam Higginbottom University, India, and a Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from ELTE (Eötvös Loránd University), Budapest, Hungary, in February 2024. From 2020 to 2024, I worked as a Programming Languages Lab Instructor in the Department of Programming Languages and Compilers at ELTE, where I gained experience in programming languages and compiler-related concepts. Prior to this, I served as a Teaching Assistant at Northern Technical University in Kirkuk, Iraq, from 2015 to 2019. My research focuses on identifying security vulnerabilities in Java applications through static analysis techniques. I have strong expertise in Java and C++, along with experience in software development, secure software engineering, static code analysis, and compiler technologies. My interests include developing reliable, high-quality software and advancing software security through effective analysis methods.
